Terms of Use

The rules everyone on BizCareers agrees to.

Draft for ReviewUpdated 28 October 2026

Who Can Use BizCareers

BizCareers is for Pakistani nationals and people who live in Pakistan. You must be at least 14 to look for work and 18 to hire.

One Account per Person

Use your real name. One email is one account. You can find jobs and hire from the same account by switching profiles.

Real Jobs Only

Every job must be a real opening in Pakistan. A job may never ask applicants for money: no registration fee, deposit, training fee or payment of any kind. Jobs that do are removed and the account closed.

The Match Score

Scores are worked out from what the job asks for and what your profile says. They help sort applications. They never reject anyone, and employers see every application.

Gender and Age Rules

An employer may set a job for men or women only, or for an age range. BizCareers checks this against what you entered, and you cannot apply to a job outside its rules.

The Verified Badge

The verified badge means BizCare staff checked a photo of the person’s CNIC. It does not mean BizCare vouches for an employer’s offer. Always meet in a safe place.

Your Content

You own what you add: your profile, CV, jobs and answers. You let BizCareers show it to the people it is meant for, such as the employers you apply to or the people looking for jobs.

Reporting

Report any job or person that looks wrong. BizCare looks at every report within 24 hours and may remove content or close accounts.

BizCare’s Role

BizCareers puts job seekers and employers in touch. BizCare is not the employer, and is not part of any offer, contract or pay agreed between them.

Closing Accounts

You can delete your account at any time. BizCare may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, and will tell you why.

The Law

These terms follow the laws of Pakistan. Any dispute is decided by the courts in Lahore.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before these terms change.

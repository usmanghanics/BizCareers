Privacy Policy

What we keep about you, and who sees it.

Draft for ReviewUpdated 28 October 2026

Who We Are

BizCareers is an app and website for finding work and hiring in Pakistan, run by BizCare Software Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd., Lahore. This policy covers both.

What We Keep

Your name, email, password (scrambled so nobody can read it), your phone number and photo if you add them, the profile or company details you add, your CV if you upload one, and your applications and jobs.

Photos and Files

The app opens your camera or gallery only when you choose to add a profile photo or your CNIC photos, and sends only the picture you pick. Your CV is a PDF file you choose to upload.

What Employers See

Your name, headline, city, experience, skills, education, languages, CV and answers. Your email and phone only after they shortlist you.

What Employers Never See

Your gender, date of birth and expected pay. These are used only to check a job’s rules and to match you.

Your CNIC

Used only to verify you. The photos are seen by BizCare staff, kept encrypted, and deleted once the check is done.

Notifications

Your phone’s notification token is used only to send you BizCareers updates, and is removed when you sign out.

Who Else Handles It

BizCare Software Solutions runs BizCareers and keeps its data on its own servers. Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone, and our email provider carries the codes we email you. Nobody else gets your information, and we never sell it.

How It Is Kept Safe

Your information travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored scrambled. CVs and CNIC photos are stored encrypted and are never on a public web page.

How Long We Keep It

As long as your account is open. When you delete it, everything listed above is removed straight away, and server backups that still hold it are overwritten within 30 days.

Deleting Your Account

Delete your account from Settings, under Account Details, and your profiles, jobs and applications are removed.

You can also ask us to do it by email. See Delete Your Account.

Children

BizCareers is for people aged 14 and over. Hiring is only for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before this policy changes.

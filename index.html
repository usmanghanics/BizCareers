Safety

Built to Keep Hiring Honest

The rules are simple, and everyone on BizCareers agrees to them.

Verified Badge

BizCare staff check a photo of the person’s CNIC, front and back, before the badge appears.

No Fee Jobs

A job may never ask applicants for money. Jobs that do are removed and the account is closed.

Reports in 24 Hours

Report any job or person that looks wrong. BizCare looks at every report within 24 hours.

Private by Default

Employers never see your gender, age or expected pay. Your phone and email show only after they shortlist you.

Questions

Good to Know

Who can use BizCareers?

Pakistani nationals and people who live in Pakistan. You must be at least 14 to look for work and 18 to hire.

Can I find work and hire from the same account?

Yes. One email is one account, with a profile for each side. Switch between them from your profile.

Does a low match score mean I am rejected?

No. The score only sorts the list. Employers see every application, and you see the same reasons they do.

What does the verified badge mean?

BizCare staff checked a photo of the person’s CNIC. It does not mean BizCare vouches for an employer’s offer, so always meet in a safe place.

When can employers see my phone number?

Only after they shortlist you. Before that they see your profile, CV and answers.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your Profile, tap the gear, then Account Details, then Delete Account. You can also ask us by email.

When can I download it?

BizCareers is coming to Google Play first. The iPhone version follows.

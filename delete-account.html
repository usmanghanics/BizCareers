Delete Your Account

You can delete your BizCareers account in the app at any time, or ask us to do it for you.

BizCareers, by BizCare Software Solutions

In the App

  1. Open BizCareers and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right.
  3. Open Account Details.
  4. Tap Delete Account, enter your password and tap Delete for Good.

Without the App

Email info@bizcare.pk from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete My Account. We reply to check it is you, then delete the account and tell you once it is done.

What Is Deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Both profiles, your photo and your CV
  • Jobs you posted, with the applications they received
  • Applications you sent and jobs you saved
  • Your notifications and your phone’s notification token

What Is Kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. CNIC photos are already deleted once the check is done. Server backups that still hold your data are overwritten within 30 days.
